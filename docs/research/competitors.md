# Competitor & feature teardown (Nov 2026)

## Global job trackers
| Product | What they nail | Gap for Bangladesh | Steal / adapt |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Simplify** | Auto-logs applications via autofill extension; Gmail status suggestions; pipeline flow chart with conversion rates; iPhone app | No govt/BCS workflow, no Bangla, no Teletalk fees/admit cards, US ATS focused | Inbox-based status suggestions (v3 Gmail), pipeline funnel chart (v2 stats), CSV import |
| **Teal** | Unlimited free tracking; capture from 40+ boards; contacts, checklists, follow-up templates; resume builder + keyword match | Web-first, English only, $13/week pricing impossible in BD | Per-job checklist, keyword match, multiple resume versions |
| **Huntr** | Kanban board, map view, contacts, interview dates, best mobile app, AI tailoring | 100-job free cap, $40/mo | Kanban UX, activity log, interview dates on cards |

## Bangladesh landscape
| Product | Category | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| Bdjobs | Job board (private), CV bank | Doesn't track govt circulars or user's multi-source pipeline |
| Teletalk Alljobs / BPSC portals | Official application portals | Per-recruitment silos; no reminders across portals |
| Circular aggregator sites/apps (bdgovtnotice, bdgovernmentjobs, many Play apps) | Listings + PDFs | Ad-heavy, no personal tracking, no reminders tied to *your* applications |
| BCS prep apps (BCS Question Bank, BCS Mate, Ogroshor) | MCQ/mock | Prep only |
| Facebook groups | Community updates (seat plan, results) | Noise, misinformation, no structure |

## Positioning
"Bdjobs/Teletalk e circular khujo, ChakriTrack e manage koro." — complement, not compete.

## Most advanced features to differentiate (ranked by impact × feasibility)
1. AI circular capture (Bangla photo → structured deadlines/fees/age) with confidence + review.
2. Fee-window & deadline reminder ladder with offline local notifications + SMS for critical.
3. Teletalk-ready photo/signature vault (resizer also as free SEO web tool).
4. Eligibility + age-out countdown.
5. Follow-a-circular verified updates (admit card/seat plan/result) — community network effect.
6. Prep tracker with FSRS + BCS syllabus weighting, streaks with freezes.
7. Mock viva AI personalised to post/district/cadre choice.
8. CV studio with ATS keyword score (honest heuristic).
